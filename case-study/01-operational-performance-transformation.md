# Operational Performance Transformation — Anonymized Case Study

## Context

An anonymized service organization needed to improve visibility and management of operational performance.

The challenge was not simply the absence of a dashboard. The underlying issue was the need to establish a coherent chain between **activity data, process logic, KPI definitions, performance review and management action**.

## Starting Point

The project identified several needs:

- Clarify the operating and customer journey.
- Consolidate fragmented operational information.
- Establish common KPI definitions.
- Distinguish activity, funnel and outcome indicators.
- Introduce recurring performance reporting.
- Make performance trends visible.
- Create a structured approach to team performance development.

## Transformation Approach

**Structure → Measure → Challenge → Coach → Adjust → Monitor**

### 1. Structure

The operating flow was clarified from demand / lead through qualification, service pathway, conversion, follow-up and performance monitoring.

### 2. Define

A KPI framework was established covering:
- activity indicators
- qualification
- gross funnel conversion
- enrollment / outcome indicators
- operational performance
- reporting and management actions

### 3. Consolidate

Data previously used in fragmented operational follow-up was organized into a consolidated performance view.

The objective was to create a single management language around definitions, periods, owners and trends.

### 4. Measure

Weekly and monthly monitoring was established.

A **13-week conversion tracking model** was used to identify fluctuations, detect performance gaps and support targeted improvement actions.

### 5. Challenge & Coach

Performance discussions moved beyond reporting the number.

The management cycle included:
- identifying gaps
- understanding contributing factors
- discussing the performance trend with the team
- identifying improvement needs
- adjusting sales / follow-up practices
- monitoring the effect of actions

### 6. Govern the KPI Logic

A key data-quality decision was to distinguish **gross conversion** from net conversion.

The operational funnel could include leads transferred to other service pathways. Therefore, the gross conversion indicator was used for operational performance monitoring rather than presenting it as final net business conversion.

### 7. Interpret Performance in Context

Individual performance was monitored as a trend rather than used for simplistic ranking because acquisition channels differed between team members.

This prevented channel differences from being incorrectly interpreted as individual capability differences.

## Performance Evidence

The three-month internal monitoring cycle showed:

**Gross conversion: +2.5 percentage points, approximately +42% relative**

Underlying lead volumes, revenue, individual performance and detailed commercial data are intentionally excluded from this public case study.

## Management System Created

The project established a repeatable cycle:

**Plan → Capture → Consolidate → Measure → Review → Challenge → Act → Monitor**

Key outputs included:

- operating flow / process structure
- KPI framework
- KPI definitions
- performance dashboard
- weekly tracking
- monthly reporting
- performance review routines
- targeted improvement actions

## PMO Perspective

The project demonstrates practical experience in:

- operational structuring
- KPI governance
- data interpretation
- performance management
- process improvement
- cross-functional coordination
- team coaching and performance development
- management reporting
- continuous improvement

## Lessons Learned

### 1. A dashboard is not the management system

The value comes from connecting data to interpretation, ownership and action.

### 2. KPI definitions matter as much as KPI values

Without clear inclusion / exclusion rules, a number can create misleading conclusions.

### 3. Context matters

Performance comparisons should consider channel, process and operating conditions.

### 4. Improvement requires a feedback loop

**Measure → Discuss → Act → Re-measure** is more useful than reporting a result once.

## Confidentiality

This case study intentionally excludes company identity, customer information, revenue figures, lead volumes, individual employee performance and proprietary operating documents.
